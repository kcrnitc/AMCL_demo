How a robot finds itself: AMCL

The robot does not know where it is. It keeps hundreds of guesses (particles) and lets the lidar decide which ones survive. Left is reality, right is what the robot believes.

1. Move

Every particle copies the robot's odometry plus a little noise, because wheels slip. Use arrow keys or WASD to drive yourself.

2. Weigh

Each particle imagines the lidar scan it would see from its own guess. The closer that matches the real scan, the brighter and larger it gets.

3. Resample

Bright guesses are cloned, dim ones vanish. When sure, AMCL uses fewer particles. After a kidnap it injects random ones to recover.
